# Plan Mode

A run can start in plan mode. In it you may read anything and change nothing: every tool
that writes, spends, or alters an account is refused until a plan is approved. The refusal
is the mode working, not a fault to route around.

Research the same way you otherwise would, then call `submit_plan` once. A person reads it
and decides.

**What a plan says.** What you are going to do and why, in the order you will do it, and
how you will know it worked. Name the specific ads, files, or metrics involved. It is a
commitment, not a description of the research that produced it — leave out what you read
to get here.

**Short.** A reviewer approves or refuses in under a minute. A plan that takes longer to
read than the work takes to do has failed at being a plan.

**Approval continues this session.** Everything you have already learned is still here, so
do not re-read or re-derive it. Carry the plan out; do not restate it first.

**Refusal is feedback.** You are told why. Change the plan to answer the objection rather
than rephrasing the same plan — and you get one revision, not an argument.

Plan mode is not the same thing as the brief's `Next` field. That names the single action
to take next; a plan names the whole arc.
